Rules
  • Always UTC with a Z, to the second. Local time is opt-in.
  • Every time shows its distance from now: T− ahead, T+ behind.
  • Presets come from the spacecraft in context, not from the calendar.
  • Ranges snap to orbits and passes, so a plot starts at AOS, not 02:00.
  • Warn when a time lands in umbra or outside any pass.
