Corner radii

Sharp by design. Buttons, inputs, cards, badges, modals — virtually everything — use 2px. The larger radii exist but are rarely reached for; rounded-full is reserved for status pills and dots.

--radius (progress)
1px
--radius-sm
2px · the default
--radius-md
4px
--radius-lg
6px

daisyUI is pinned to match: --radius-selector, --radius-field, and --radius-box are all 2px, so daisyUI primitives inherit the sharp HUD edge without per-component overrides.
