Data viz · charts

◎ Grounded in the real telemetry_chart.js hook. Tags mark current vs proposed.

Cadence is a telemetry product — the time-series strip chart is its core surface, not an afterthought. The governing rule: a series' color identifies which signal it is; a value's color says how it's doing. Those two jobs must never share a hue, or a red line becomes ambiguous — is it "series 4" or "in red limit"?

Limit overlays on a chart

Threshold lines current — red limits solid, yellow limits dashed, at the limit value. Caution-band shading proposed — a faint fill (status / 0.06) between thresholds so the safe corridor reads at a glance without staring at the y-axis. Keep both subtle: the data line is the figure, the limits are ground.

Numbers & time

Values are monospace with tabular-nums (use .mc-value-*), fixed per-point precision, unit as a dim suffix. Time is always UTC, HH:MM:SSZ on axes and YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ in detail. Never let digits reflow — tabular figures keep a streaming value from twitching.

Usage

  • ✓ Color a series by identity from the palette above; color a value by its limit state. Never the same hue for both jobs.
  • ✓ Keep limits as ground (thin lines, faint bands) and the signal as figure — the data line is always the most prominent mark.
  • ✓ Secondary / compare traces are slate, thinner (1.1), dashed — visibly subordinate to the live series.
  • ✗ Don't put red, yellow, green, or info-blue in the identity palette — those hues are spoken for by limits.
  • ✗ Don't solve "many series" with more colors — past ~6, vary lightness/dash or break into small multiples.
